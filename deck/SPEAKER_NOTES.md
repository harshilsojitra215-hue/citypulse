## Slide 7
Think of CityPulse as a team of five. The collector searches news, city pages, council papers and video comments, and brings every item in with its link and its real date. The reader reads it and asks: is this about the cable car, does someone take a side, and what is it about? That is classification and claim extraction, done by a language model, Claude Haiku. ## Slide 10
Backup slide, open only if someone asks how it works technically. Walk it left to right, top row then bottom row. Information enters from two places: the city's two years of data and our live collector. First we clean and select: Python rules and Claude Haiku check relevance, remove duplicates and decide which project a post is about. Then Haiku understands the text: the stance, the claims, the argument. To find similar arguments, an embedding model called bge-m3 turns the text into numbers that capture its meaning. Then our own fine-tuned model compares claim pairs and decides whether two claims make the same argument. From that we decide whether a concern is new, familiar or uncertain, and we flag it when two or more independent voices raise it. Then we calculate and verify: counts, trends, tests, always with the original evidence and the method. And it all ends on the dashboard: the historical replay, Ask CityPulse and the live updates.

*Source:* Clean and select: pipeline/01_build_base.py, 02_candidates.py (keyword rules), dedupe by text, Haiku project label (engine/citypulse/batch/steps.py). Understand: one Haiku call per 20 posts with codebook_v1 returns project, stance and arguments. Embeddings: bge-m3 through Ollama on the laptop's graphics card (same vectors as the engine's stored ones, cosine 1.0). Matcher: citypulse-matcher-v2, mDeBERTa base fine-tuned on claim pairs, F1 0.65 vs 0.37 for plain similarity against an AI reference (Claude Opus), not human-checked; contradiction spotting is switched off. Emerging: familiar above 0.80, new below 0.29, in between = uncertain, needs review (app_data/matcher.json). Statistics: Wilson intervals on shares, peak weeks against a placebo test, exact tests in Ask. Dashboard: app/ (History, So far, Today, Human review, Ask CityPulse).
